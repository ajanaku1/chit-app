# Ideas checked against the code, 21 October 2026

The founder's two rounds of ideas, each checked against what the bot already has. Rows R75 to R89 are in [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md); this page holds the detail. Sizes are build days for one person, not dates. Nothing here moves agents (R67, R68) off first place; R75 to R78 are small enough to run beside it.

## Order

1. R75 sell test before buy, with the tax on the card. The strongest trust story, and it backs the rug shield.
2. R76 panic button and R77 position alerts.
3. R78 Chit in any group.
4. R86 buyback-as-a-service, as a contract design and review first.
5. R89 is a read, not a feature, and should happen before R86 or any new trade path.

The TP ladder ("sell 25% at +50, 25% at +100, 50% at +200" in one message) is not a row: it is one sentence with three exits, which R67 already places on one confirm card.

## Not built, on purpose

- **Gift a bag** (buy a token into a friend's account). It would depend on the same unchecked recipient as R89, the path a stolen key would use. If R89 closes it, gifting goes with it; a gift could come back later as a sell-and-send from the owner's own wallet.
